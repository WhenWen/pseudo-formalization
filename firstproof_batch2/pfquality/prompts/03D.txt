\sum_{i=1}^m w_i(1)

<LEMMA_PROOF id="2.3">

\[
1
=
\Pr\Big[\bigcup_{j=1}^k A_j\Big]
\le
\sum_{j=1}^k \Pr[A_j]
=
k\Pr[A_1].
\]
Dividing by \(k\) gives \(\Pr[A_1]\ge 1/k\).
</LEMMA_PROOF>
