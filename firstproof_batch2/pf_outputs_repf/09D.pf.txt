<THEOREM_STATEMENT id="1">
Let \(m\) and \(n\) be positive integers, and let \(R_n^{(m)}\) be the coinvariant algebra with \(m\) sets of \(n\) variables. Its \(m\)-multigraded Hilbert series is written
\[
\mathrm{Hilb}(R_n^{(m)};q_1,\ldots,q_m)=\sum_\lambda c_\lambda(n)s_\lambda(q_1,\ldots,q_m),
\]
where the coefficients \(c_\lambda(n)\) are universal nonnegative coefficients independent of \(m\). Let \(\lambda=(k,1^d)\) be a hook shape partition with \(k\geq 1\) and \(d\geq 0\), and let
\[
SR_n=
\mathbb{C}[x_1,\dots,x_n]\otimes \Lambda(\theta_1,\dots,\theta_n)/
\left\langle p_r(x),\sum_i x_i^{r-1}\theta_i\right\rangle .
\]
Statement :
The universal hook coefficient \(c_{(k,1^d)}(n)\) enumerates the number of Ordered Set Partitions of \([n]\) that act as highest-weight sources, meaning elements annihilated by the fermionic derivation operator in the OSP combinatorial basis of \(SR_n\), possessing polynomial degree \(k\) and exterior degree \(d\). For \(d=0\), this interpretation collapses to counting permutations in \(S_n\) by major index \(k\), yielding the Mahonian distribution \(c_{(k)}(n)\).
</THEOREM_STATEMENT>

<PROPOSITION_STATEMENT id="1">
Let \(m,n\) be positive integers. Let \(V\) be the \((n-1)\)-dimensional standard representation of \(S_n\), and let \(E=\mathbb{C}^m\). Let \(I\) be the ideal generated by the positive-degree multi-symmetric invariants.
Statement :
The polynomial ring representing \(m\) sets of \(n\) variables is
\[
\mathbb{C}[X]=\operatorname{Sym}(V\otimes E),
\]
and the multi-graded coinvariant algebra is
\[
R_n^{(m)}=\operatorname{Sym}(V\otimes E)/I.
\]
</PROPOSITION_STATEMENT>

<PROPOSITION_PROOF id="1">
None
</PROPOSITION_PROOF>

<PROPOSITION_STATEMENT id="2">
Let \(m,n\) be positive integers, let \(V\) be the \((n-1)\)-dimensional standard representation of \(S_n\), let \(E=\mathbb{C}^m\), and let
\[
R_n^{(m)}=\operatorname{Sym}(V\otimes E)/I
\]
where \(I\) is generated by the positive-degree multi-symmetric invariants.
Statement :
The \(GL(E)\)-character of \(R_n^{(m)}\) decomposes as
\[
\sum_\lambda c_\lambda(n)s_\lambda(E),
\]
where the coefficients \(c_\lambda(n)\) are universal nonnegative integers that do not depend on \(m\), provided \(m\geq \ell(\lambda)\).
</PROPOSITION_STATEMENT>

<PROPOSITION_PROOF id="2">
By Cauchy's formula,
\[
\operatorname{Sym}(V\otimes E)
\]
decomposes as a \(GL(V)\times GL(E)\)-module into
\[
\bigoplus_\lambda S^\lambda(V)\otimes S^\lambda(E).
\]
Because the quotient ideal \(I\) from Proposition 1 is \(S_n\)-invariant, the \(GL(E)\)-character of the quotient decomposes with the same Schur labels and with nonnegative multiplicities independent of \(m\) in the stable range \(m\geq \ell(\lambda)\).

External citations: Cauchy's formula.
</PROPOSITION_PROOF>

<PROPOSITION_STATEMENT id="3">
Let \(E=\mathbb{C}^{1|1}\) be the superspace with one commuting variable \(q\) and one anti-commuting variable \(z\).
Statement :
For this superspace, the Schur functor \(S^\lambda(\mathbb{C}^{1|1})\) evaluates to zero for all non-hook shapes \(\lambda\).
</PROPOSITION_STATEMENT>

<PROPOSITION_PROOF id="3">
This is the specialization property of Schur functors on the superspace \(\mathbb{C}^{1|1}\).
</PROPOSITION_PROOF>

<PROPOSITION_STATEMENT id="4">
Let \(R_n^{(m)}=\operatorname{Sym}(V\otimes E)/I\), where \(I\) is generated by the positive-degree multi-symmetric invariants, and evaluate the \(GL_m\)-character at the superspace \(E=\mathbb{C}^{1|1}\).
Statement :
The generators of \(I\), namely the polarizations of the power sums \(p_r\), map directly to
\[
p_r(x)
\]
and to their fermionic derivations
\[
\sum_i x_i^{r-1}\theta_i.

</PROPOSITION_STATEMENT>

<PROPOSITION_PROOF id="4">
The commuting coordinate of \(\mathbb{C}^{1|1}\) gives the ordinary power sums \(p_r(x)\), while the anti-commuting coordinate gives the corresponding fermionic derivations \(\sum_i x_i^{r-1}\theta_i\).
</PROPOSITION_PROOF>

<PROPOSITION_STATEMENT id="5">
Let \(R_n^{(m)}=\operatorname{Sym}(V\otimes E)/I\), and evaluate at \(E=\mathbb{C}^{1|1}\). Let

<PROPOSITION_STATEMENT id="6">
Let \(\lambda=(k,1^d)\) be a hook shape partition. Let \(c_{(k,1^d)}(n)\) be the universal coefficient of \(s_{(k,1^d)}\) in the \(m\)-multigraded Hilbert series of \(R_n^{(m)}\).
Statement :
The coefficient \(c_{(k,1^d)}(n)\) is exactly the multiplicity of the super-Schur module
\[
S^{(k,1^d)}(\mathbb{C}^{1|1})
\]
in the super-coinvariant algebra \(SR_n\).
</PROPOSITION_STATEMENT>

<PROPOSITION_PROOF id="6">
Proposition 2 gives the universal Schur decomposition of the \(GL(E)\)-character. Proposition 3 isolates the hook shapes after evaluation at \(E=\mathbb{C}^{1|1}\). Proposition 5 identifies the evaluated quotient with \(SR_n\). Therefore the surviving hook coefficient is the multiplicity of the corresponding super-Schur module in \(SR_n\).
</PROPOSITION_PROOF>

<PROPOSITION_STATEMENT id="9">
Let \(\lambda=(k,1^d)\) be a hook shape partition. Let \(c_{(k,1^d)}(n)\) be the universal hook coefficient in the \(m\)-multigraded Hilbert series of \(R_n^{(m)}\). In the OSP combinatorial basis of \(SR_n\), call an element a highest-weight source if it is annihilated by the fermionic derivation operator.
Statement :
The coefficient \(c_{(k,1^d)}(n)\) enumerates the number of Ordered Set Partitions of \([n]\) that act as highest-weight sources possessing polynomial degree \(k\) and exterior degree \(d\).
</PROPOSITION_STATEMENT>

<PROPOSITION_PROOF id="9">
By Proposition 6, \(c_{(k,1^d)}(n)\) is the multiplicity of \(S^{(k,1^d)}(\mathbb{C}^{1|1})\) in \(SR_n\). By Proposition 7, each such hook module is spanned by a highest-weight vector and its lowering derivation. By Proposition 8, \(SR_n\) is represented by the OSP combinatorial basis. Counting the highest-weight source elements in that basis with polynomial degree \(k\) and exterior degree \(d\) gives the stated coefficient.
</PROPOSITION_PROOF>

<PROPOSITION_STATEMENT id="10">
Let \(d=0\), so the hook shape is \((k)\).
Statement :
The interpretation of \(c_{(k)}(n)\) collapses to counting permutations in \(S_n\) by major index \(k\), yielding the exact Mahonian distribution.
</PROPOSITION_STATEMENT>

<PROPOSITION_PROOF id="10">
For \(d=0\), the exterior degree is zero, which strictly filters the domain to Ordered Set Partitions possessing exactly \(n\) blocks. An Ordered Set Partition of \([n]\) into exactly \(n\) blocks is bijectively a permutation in \(S_n\). Using Proposition 9, the resulting count is by polynomial degree \(k\), identified in this collapse with major index \(k\).
</PROPOSITION_PROOF>
