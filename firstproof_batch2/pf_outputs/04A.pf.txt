<THEOREM_STATEMENT id="1">
- \(R=\prod_{i=1}^4[0,R_i]\) and \(S=\prod_{i=1}^4[0,S_i]\) are oriented Euclidean rectangles, with side lengths labeled in the usual increasing order.
- \(f:(R,\partial R)\to(S,\partial S)\) is a smooth transverse map of pairs of relative degree \(1\), meaning degree \(1\) on \(H_4(R,\partial R;\mathbb Z)\to H_4(S,\partial S;\mathbb Z)\).
- \(\Dil_2(f)\le1\). All constants are positive and universal.
- Let \(Q\) be the concentric subrectangle of the \((x_3,x_4)\)-face of \(S\) with side lengths \(S_3/2,S_4/2\), and set
\[
P_y=[0,S_1]\times[0,S_2]\times\{y\},\qquad y\in Q.
\]
- In the hard-case discussion, let \(F=(f_3,f_4)\), \(G=(f_1,f_2)\), \(Z_y=\langle [[R]],F,y\rangle\),
\[
E_y=\int_{Z_y}\|dG|_{TZ_y}\|_{HS}^2\,d\|Z_y\|,\qquad
I=\int_QE_y\,dy,\qquad q=|Q|,
\]
and \(\Omega=\{y\in Q:E_y\le4I/q\}\).

First, there is \(c_2>0\) such that, if
\[
R_1R_2^2\le c_2S_1S_2S_3,
\]
then
\[
R_3R_4\ge c_2S_3S_4.
\]
Second, if \(R_1=\alpha S_1\le S_1\) and the first conclusion fails with a small constant, then unconditionally
\[
\Vol(R)\ge cS_1S_2^{3/5}S_3^{7/5}S_4.
\]
Third, assuming the averaged tightening estimate \((\mathrm{EAT})\) stated in Proposition 8, if \(R_3R_4\le\kappa S_3S_4\) and \(R_1\le\kappa S_1\), then
\[
I\ge cS_1S_2^{1/2}S_3^{3/2}S_4
\]
and hence
\[
\Vol(R)\ge cS_1S_2^{1/2}S_3^{3/2}S_4.
\]
Thus, assuming \((\mathrm{EAT})\), the desired hard-case second alternative follows. Without \((\mathrm{EAT})\), the document is a conditional reduction, not a complete proof of the stated dichotomy.
</THEOREM_STATEMENT>

\le C\left(R_1I+\frac{I^2}{S_1q}\right)

<PROPOSITION_PROOF id="8">

<PROPOSITION_STATEMENT id="10">
- Consider a two-cell model with two oriented \(2\)-cells \(A,A'\) satisfying \(f_\#A=f_\#A'=P\).
- Let \(N=A-A'\).
- Suppose an exact-plaque bad set contains \(A\) but not \(A'\), and let \(P_{\rm bad}\) be the source-local projection with \(P_{\rm bad}A=A\), \(P_{\rm bad}A'=0\).

In the two-cell model,
\[
N\in\ker f_\#,
\qquad
f_\#P_{\rm bad}N=P\ne0.
\]
</PROPOSITION_STATEMENT>

<PROPOSITION_PROOF id="10">
Since \(f_\#A=f_\#A'=P\), one has \(f_\#N=0\). But \(P_{\rm bad}N=A\), so \(f_\#P_{\rm bad}N=P\ne0\). Dually, a quotient cochain has equal values on \(A\) and \(A'\), and restriction to the nonsaturated bad set changes one value but not the other. Thus exact source localization and quotient invariance are in tension.
</PROPOSITION_PROOF>

<THEOREM_PROOF id="1">
The proof is written for smooth transverse maps; piecewise smooth maps may be treated by the same current-slicing argument, or by smoothing on strata with an arbitrarily small \(C^1\)-loss and then absorbing that loss into the universal constants.

Proposition 3 proves the first alternative. Proposition 4 proves the unconditional partial hard-case bound. Proposition 9 proves the desired second hard-case alternative assuming \((\mathrm{EAT})\). Proposition 10 records why the remaining estimate is the essential unresolved gap and why the document is a conditional reduction rather than a complete proof.
</THEOREM_PROOF>
