<THEOREM_STATEMENT id="1">
Assumptions / Conditions / Definitions.
- \(m,n\) are positive integers.
- \(R_n^{(m)}\) is the coinvariant algebra with \(m\) sets of \(n\) variables.
- Its Hilbert series has the Schur expansion
\[
\mathrm{Hilb}(R_n^{(m)};q_1,\ldots,q_m)=\sum_{\lambda}c_\lambda(n)s_\lambda(q_1,\ldots,q_m),
\]
where the coefficients \(c_\lambda(n)\) are universal non-negative coefficients independent of \(m\).
- Let \(\lambda=(k,1^d)\) be a hook partition, with \(k\geq 1\) and \(d\geq 0\).
- The source presents this as a partial attempt; the automated verification pipeline did not confirm a complete solution.

Statement :
The proposed combinatorial interpretation is that \(c_{(k,1^d)}(n)\) exactly enumerates the number of Ordered Set Partitions of \([n]\) that act as highest-weight ``sources'', namely elements annihilated by the fermionic derivation operator in the OSP combinatorial basis of \(SR_n\), possessing exactly polynomial degree \(k\) and exterior degree \(d\).

For \(d=0\), the exterior degree zero condition filters the domain to Ordered Set Partitions of \([n]\) with exactly \(n\) blocks; these are bijective with permutations in \(S_n\), so the interpretation collapses to counting permutations by major index \(k\), yielding the Mahonian distribution \(c_{(k)}(n)\).
</THEOREM_STATEMENT>

<PROPOSITION_STATEMENT id="1">

- \(E=\mathbb{C}^m\).
- \(I\) is the ideal generated by the positive-degree multi-symmetric invariants.

Statement :
The polynomial model
\[
\mathbb{C}[X]=\operatorname{Sym}(V\otimes E),\qquad
R_n^{(m)}=\operatorname{Sym}(V\otimes E)/I
