--- Lemma 1.1 ---
STATEMENT: Assumptions / Conditions / Definitions.
- Let \(X_1,X_2,\ldots\) be i.i.d. real-valued random variables.
- Let \(m\ge1\) and \(w_1,\ldots,w_m\ge0\) satisfy \(\sum_{i=1}^{m}w_i=1\).

Statement :
For every real \(u\),
\[
\Pr\!\left[\sum_{i=1}^{m}w_iX_i>u\right]
\ge
\inf_{N\ge1}
\Pr\!\left[\frac{X_1+\cdots+X_N}{N}>u\right].
\]
PROOF: This is the one-dimensional half-line form of Hoeffding's extremal theorem for convex combinations of i.i.d. random variables.

External citations: W. Hoeffding, \emph{The extrema of the expected value of a function of independent random variables}, Annals of Mathematical Statistics \textbf{26} (1955), 268--275.
