Its DKEP support projection \(q_X\in\mathcal N\) is the projection obtained as the weak-* limit in \(\mathcal N\) of any contractive approximate identity of \(X\), equivalently the least projection supporting the weak-* closure of \(X\). The compact support \(q_K\in\mathcal N\) is the support projection of \(K(H)\).
