The PF preserves the core mathematical theorem, the Guth product estimate, the definitions of \(X,I,Y\), the choice of \(\kappa\), and the case split proving the final alternative. I do **not** see a substantive change in the main hypotheses, quantifiers, inequalities, or the strict/non-strict conclusions.

However, as a whole-proof/document preservation check, there are several divergences.

### 1. Front matter and abstract are dropped / only partially relocated

**Author text:**
```latex
\title{Submission C solution to Problem 4}
\date{}
\maketitle
```

and the abstract:

> “We prove the stated estimate as an immediate consequence of Guth's product-domain form of his area-contraction estimates for rectangles. All constants below are positive dimensional constants; in this problem the dimension is fixed equal to \(4\), so the final constant is universal.”

**PF:** No title, date, maketitle, or abstract appears. The PF only includes, inside the theorem proof:

> “Since the dimension is fixed equal to \(4\), this \(\kappa\) is universal.”

**Why it matters:**  
The PF retains the mathematical fact that \(\kappa\) is universal, but it drops the author’s framing that the proof is an “immediate consequence” of Guth’s product-domain area-contraction estimates, and it drops the explicit statement that “all constants below are positive dimensional constants.” This is contextual rather than a change to the proof’s logical core, but it is author content not fully preserved.
