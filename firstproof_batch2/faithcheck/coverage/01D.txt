## Findings

### 1. PF drops much of the author’s explicit definition of “representation”

**Author text:**  
> “A representation \(\A\) of such a structure is an instance of its isomorphism type in which the universe or domain of the structure is the set of natural numbers \(\N\) and the constants, functions and relations of the structure are elements of \(\N\), multi-variable functions from \(\N\) to \(\N\) and multi-place relations on \(\N\), respectively.”

**PF:**  
> “A representation \(\mathcal A\) of an infinite countable first-order structure has universe \(\mathbb N\). Its automorphism group is denoted \(\AUT(\mathcal A)\).”

**Issue:**  
The PF keeps only the universe/domain part and drops the author’s explicit coding of constants, functions, and relations as natural numbers/functions/relations on \(\mathbb N\). This matters because the proof uses notions like “computable relative to \(\mathcal A\)” and “atomic diagram,” whose meaning depends on how the represented structure is coded.

### 2. PF drops the author’s explicit definition of automorphism/isomorphism

**Author text:**

### 3. PF drops the first bullet of Definition 1.1: “computably \(\AUT\)-countable”

**Author text:**

The PF includes only the cone version:

There is no PF counterpart for the non-cone definition.

**Issue:**  
Even though the proof mainly uses the cone version, the author explicitly stated both definitions. The PF is incomplete as a rendering of the full original text.

### 8. PF adds/generalises some intermediate claims beyond the author’s exact statements
