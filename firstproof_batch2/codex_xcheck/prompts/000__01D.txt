== ERROR ==
title: Misstated Ash--Knight--Manasse--Slaman--Chisholm theorem omits uniformity
type / severity: false_claim / fatal
description: The quoted theorem is false as stated. The parameter-free version of the Ash--Knight--Manasse--Slaman--Chisholm theorem requires that the copies $f(R)$ be computable uniformly relative to $N\oplus C$, i.e. by a single fixed procedure working for all copies, not merely that each $f(R)$ be computable relative to its own oracle in a nonuniform way. Without this uniformity one only obtains a different, parameter-allowing/nonuniform form of definability, and the claimed parameter-free $L_{\omega_1\omega}$ $\Sigma_1$ definition does not follow. Since the proof’s contradiction depends entirely on this theorem producing such a definition, the argument collapses.

== ORIGINAL TEXT THE AUTHOR WROTE (the errored span) ==
\begin{theorem*}[Ash--Knight--Manasse--Slaman and Chisholm]
Let $M$ be a countable first-order structure, and let $R$ be a relation on the domain of $M$. Suppose that $R$ is invariant under all automorphisms of $M$. Suppose there exists an oracle $C \subset \mathbb{N}$ such that for every structure $N$ with domain $\mathbb{N}$ and every isomorphism $f: M \to N$, the relation $f(R)$ is computable relative to $N \oplus C$. Then $R$ is definable in $M$ by an $L_{\omega_1\omega}$ $\Sigma_1$ formula without parameters (meaning there is a countable family of parameter-free existential formulas in the language of $M$ whose disjunction defines $R$).
\end{theorem*}
