--- Lemma 4.1 ---

--- Lemma 4.2 ---
STATEMENT: Assumptions / Conditions / Definitions.
- Let \(S\) be a real-valued random variable such that
\[
\mathbb P(S>1/2)=\mathbb P(S<1/2).
\]

Statement :
\[
\mathbb P(S\ge1/2) \ge \frac12.
\]
PROOF: The events \(S<1/2\), \(S=1/2\), and \(S>1/2\) partition the probability space, and the two strict-tail probabilities are equal.
\[
\mathbb P(S\ge1/2)
=
\mathbb P(S>1/2)+\mathbb P(S=1/2)
=
\frac{1+\mathbb P(S=1/2)}2
\ge \frac12.
\]
