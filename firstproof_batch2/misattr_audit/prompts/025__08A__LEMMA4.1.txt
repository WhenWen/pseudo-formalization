--- Proposition 1 [DEPENDS ON: 10, 12, 13] ---
STATEMENT: Assumptions / Conditions / Definitions.
- Let \(p,q\) be valuated matroids on \(E\) of ranks \(a\le b\).
- All-\(\infty\) minima are regarded as attained at every displayed index.

Statement :
The standard model of the relative Dressian is the following: a rank \(k\) point is a projective valuated matroid \(\nu\) with \(\nu\preceq\mu_M\). Brandt--Eur--Zhang identify this order with inclusion of projective tropical linear spaces,
\[
        \Trop(\theta)\subseteq\Trop(\nu)
        \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad \theta\preceq\nu .
\]
For valuated matroids \(p,q\) of ranks \(a\le b\), \(p\preceq q\) is equivalent to the incidence-Plucker relations
\[
 \min_{i\in J\setminus I}\{p(I\cup i)+q(J\setminus i)\}
 \quad\hbox{attained at least twice}
\]
for all \(I\in\binom{E}{a-1}\) and \(J\in\binom{E}{b+1}\).
PROOF: This is the standard relative-Dressian quotient model and the standard incidence-Plucker characterization of valuated quotients.

External citations: M. Brandt, C. Eur and L. Zhang, “Tropical flag varieties,” Advances in Mathematics 384 (2021), 107695.

--- Lemma 2.2 ---
STATEMENT: Assumptions / Conditions / Definitions.
- In the setting of Proposition 2, fix \(J\in\binom{E}{b+1}\) and put \(c_j=q(J\setminus j)\) for \(j\in J\).

- Assume the nested incidence-Plucker relation holds for every \(S\subseteq J\) with \(|S|=a-1\).
